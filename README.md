### Hi, I'm Fahim 👋

**Agricultural Data Scientist at Syngenta Group**; Ph.D., Civil and Environmental Engineering, Colorado State University


I work at the intersection of **remote sensing, machine learning, hydrology, and agriculture** to understand agricultural water use, from single fields to regional scales. My research has explored questions related to water use efficiency, evapotranspiration, effective precipitation, groundwater pumping, and irrigation response to climate.
